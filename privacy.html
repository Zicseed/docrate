Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

DocRate is a student platform for discovering professors and courses, reading student reviews, and submitting reviews. This policy explains how DocRate at https://doc-rate.com handles personal information.

1. Who handles your information

DocRate is operated by Omar Alhafez. For privacy questions, account deletion, or requests concerning your information, contact legal@doc-rate.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier, display name, and authentication information needed to register and sign in.
  • Review information: ratings, written comments, selected professor or course, information you enter in review forms, submission dates, and moderation status.
  • Support information: messages and details you provide when contacting us or reporting content.
  • Technical information: our infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, browser and device information, request timestamps, and service or security logs.

3. Sign in with Google

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides basic account information such as your Google account identifier, email address, name, and profile image when available. We use this information to authenticate you, create or link your DocRate account, and maintain your sign-in session.

Google account information is processed through Supabase, our authentication and database provider. Account information is stored with your DocRate account. Session credentials may be stored in your browser to keep you signed in.

DocRate's Google sign-in requests basic identity permissions. It does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts, or calendar. Your Google password is entered with Google and is not provided to DocRate.

We do not sell Google account information or use it for advertising targeting. We share it only as needed to operate authentication and account services, respond to your requests, protect the service, or meet legal obligations.

You can revoke DocRate's Google access in your Google Account settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete information already stored in your DocRate account; contact us to request deletion.

4. How we use information

We use information to operate accounts, authenticate users, publish and moderate reviews, respond to support requests, prevent spam and abuse, troubleshoot problems, and protect the security of DocRate.

5. Anonymous reviews

Reviews presented as anonymous do not display the author's account name or email publicly. However, anonymous publication does not mean that the submission is disconnected from your account internally. Review records may be linked to your account for moderation, security, and account management.

Review text, ratings, and relevant course or professor details may be visible publicly after publication. Do not include private information about yourself or others in a review. Public content may be copied, shared, or indexed by search engines.

6. Providers and sharing

We use Supabase for authentication and database services, Netlify for website hosting, and Cloudflare for domain and DNS services. Google processes information when you choose Google sign-in. These providers process information relevant to their services under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Authorized administrators may access information needed for support, moderation, and security. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to investigate abuse and protect users. We do not sell account information.

When you use a social sharing button, the selected platform receives the link and processes information under its own privacy policy.

Website analytics

We use Netlify Web Analytics to understand website traffic and improve DocRate. Netlify generates these statistics from its server logs, without adding analytics tracking cookies or a tracking script to your browser.

These reports include pageviews, visitor estimates based on IP addresses, approximate visitor locations, requested pages, referring websites, and bandwidth usage. We use the reports to understand traffic trends and identify technical problems. These visitor estimates are not counts of registered accounts.

Our infrastructure providers also process technical information for hosting and security, as described above. For more information, see Netlify's Privacy Policy.

7. Browser storage

DocRate uses browser storage to maintain authentication sessions and remember settings. Clearing browser storage may sign you out or reset preferences.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep account information while it is needed to provide your account and operate the service. Review information is retained to provide published reviews and moderation. Contact us to request deletion of your account or reviews.

Some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations, investigate abuse, or resolve disputes. Copies in provider backups and logs may remain until those systems complete their retention cycles. Deleting content from DocRate cannot remove copies made by other people or search engines.

9. Security and international processing

We use access controls and authentication services to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Our service providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence.

10. Your choices and requests

You can browse public content without creating an account. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Send requests to our contact email. We may ask for information needed to verify that the account belongs to you.

11. Changes to this policy

We will publish updates on this page and change the date above. Material changes may also be communicated through the website or account contact details where appropriate.
